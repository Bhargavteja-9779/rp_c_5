## 2. Reproducibility audit (Phase 35), run as a stranger on 15 Oct 2026
1. Fresh `git clone` of the branch, with no data present.
2. `python run_all.py --mode quick` downloaded both datasets, verified SHA-256, ran all 12 experiments and generated statistics, tables and figures (~3 min).
3. `pytest`: **18 passed**.
4. The full-mode `official_split` experiment was re-run from the fresh clone and compared with the committed results: max |Δ| = 1.1e-16 (coverage), 1.4e-14 (width), 5.7e-14 (interval score). **Bit-level reproduction up to floating-point rounding.**
5. Earlier checks: the pre-specified v1 main results were reproduced exactly (198,000 rows) after methods were added, and after the weighted-CP and M7 changes all other methods remained identical (216,000 and 234,000 rows).
6. Manuscript, supplement, highlights and cover letter are regenerated by `manuscript/build_manuscript.py`, `build_supplementary.py` and `cover_letter.py`.

Not reproducible by design: wall-clock timings (Table S8, Fig. S1) vary between machines and runs.
