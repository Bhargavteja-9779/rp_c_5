# Review round 3 — Reviewer #3 (statistical validity, fairness, seeds, leakage)

**Overall:** Minor-to-major revision. The design is careful: fixed seeds, scenario-level unit of analysis, Holm correction, effect sizes. Three statistical weaknesses remain.

| # | Severity | Issue | Fix | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | MAJOR | Coverage is averaged over 100 splits. That is a marginal guarantee, but a laboratory deploys one calibration with one transfer set. How often does a single deployment under-cover badly (training-conditional coverage)? | Report the split-level distribution, with a reference for pure sampling noise. | **Fixed (new analysis).** Table S4 gives the median, 10th and 5th percentiles, and the fraction of splits below 0.80. The reference is the in-domain conformal interval. TD-CP+'s tail (corn: 4.7–9.6% of splits < 0.80) is comparable to the ideal in-domain interval (9.4%). Naive CP is below 0.80 in 54–82% of splits. Added to Section 4.3. |
| 2 | MAJOR | The 60 scenario means are not independent: corn scenarios share samples, and PDS/DS share splits. Wilcoxon p-values are optimistic (pseudo-replication at a higher level). | Acknowledge, and add a dependence-robust check. | **Fixed.** Stated explicitly in Section 4.3. Added a per-stratum direction-consistency analysis (dataset × transfer; Table S5). The main conclusions hold in every stratum (TD-CP+ beats naive and weighted CP on coverage gap in ≥ 80% of the scenarios of each stratum). |
| 3 | MODERATE | The validity tolerance of 0.02 is arbitrary. | Sensitivity analysis. | **Fixed.** Table S6 covers tolerances 0, 0.01, 0.02 and 0.03. TD-CP+ is valid in ≥ 96.7% of scenarios even at zero tolerance. The conclusions do not depend on the tolerance. |
